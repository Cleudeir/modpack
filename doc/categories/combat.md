## Combat Tips

- Use Better Combat for smoother animations
- Equip Artifacts/Trinkets for special abilities
- Enable damage numbers for combat feedback
- Use trajectory preview for ranged weapons

## Weapon Balancing

- Configure weapon damage in mod configs
- Adjust armor values with Apothic Attributes
- Use AttributeFix to prevent attribute overflow

## Quest System

FTB Quests provides:
- Main questline for progression
- Side quests for rewards
- Chapter-based organization
- Achievement tracking

## Dungeon Tips

- Prepare before entering dungeons
- Bring adequate supplies
- Watch for trap rooms
- Use sound cues for enemy detection

## Difficulty Scaling

- Zombie Extreme increases difficulty over time
- World of Rot adds decay pressure
- Blocky Siege requires base defense
- Scale Health adjusts mob difficulty

## Adventure Strategy

1. Complete main quests first
2. Explore dungeons for loot
3. Build defenses for siege events
4. Manage resources carefully

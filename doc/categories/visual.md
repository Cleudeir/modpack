## Shader Support

- **Oculus**: Forge shader loader (Iris port)
- **Embeddium**: Required for shader performance
- Compatible with most OptiFine shaders

## Visual Enhancements

- Dynamic lights for held items
- Enhanced visual effects for immersion
- Better entity textures for variety
- Realistic sound physics

## Audio Improvements

- Presence Footsteps: Unique sounds per block
- Sound Physics: Echo and reverb effects
- Ambient Sounds: Environmental audio

## Performance Impact

- Embeddium: Positive (FPS boost)
- Shaders: Varies by shader pack
- Sound mods: Minimal impact
- Animation mods: Light performance cost

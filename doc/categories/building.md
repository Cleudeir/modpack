## Building Tips

- Use Effortless Building for quick construction
- Combine mods for unique furniture styles
- Every Compat ensures wood consistency
- Use Supplementaries for vanilla+ feel

## Decoration Combinations

- **Modern**: Design Decor + Handcrafted
- **Rustic**: Supplementaries + Ecologics
- **Fantasy**: Quark + Handcrafted
- **Vanilla+**: Amendments + Supplementaries

## Creative Mode Tips

- Search by mod in creative menu
- Use Catalogue to browse all items
- Check Patchouli for building guides

# Mutant Monsters

**Version:** 1.20.1-1.0.0
**Category:** Mobs & Entities

## Description
Mutant Monsters adds powerful mutant versions of standard Minecraft mobs. These elite variants are significantly stronger than their normal counterparts and drop rare materials when defeated.

## Key Features
- Mutant Zombie with massive health and damage
- Mutant Skeleton with rapid-fire arrows
- Mutant Creeper with chain explosions
- Mutant Enderman with teleportation abilities
- Mutant Witch with enhanced potions
- Rare spawn rates and challenging encounters
- Unique loot drops for crafting

## Configuration
Config file: `config/mutantmonsters.toml`

Key settings:
- Spawn rate adjustments
- Health and damage multipliers
- Drop table customization
- Enable/disable individual mutants

## Tips
- Prepare diamond or netherite gear before hunting mutants
- Mutants have increased knockback resistance
- Some mutants spawn more frequently during blood moons
- Keep distance—their attacks have extended range

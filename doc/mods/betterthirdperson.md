# Better Third Person

**Version:** 1.x.x
**Category:** Quality of Life

## Description
Better Third Person improves the third-person camera in Minecraft, providing better angles, smoother movement, and more control over the camera position.

## Key Features
- Improved camera angles
- Smoother camera movement
- Better head tracking
- Configurable camera offsets
- Works with custom models and capes

## Configuration
Config file: `config/betterthirdperson.toml`

Key settings:
- `cameraOffset` - Distance of camera from player
- `verticalOffset` - Height offset for camera
- `smoothing` - Camera smoothing amount
- `enableHeadTracking` - Look direction affects camera

## Tips
- Press F5 to cycle through camera modes
- Adjust offsets for your preferred view
- Works well with shader packs
- Great for screenshots and exploration

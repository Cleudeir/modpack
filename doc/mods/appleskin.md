# AppleSkin

**Version:** 2.5.x
**Category:** Quality of Life

## Description
AppleSkin adds helpful HUD elements that show food saturation and exhaustion values. This helps players understand how food works and when they'll actually get hunger restored.

## Key Features
- Shows food saturation level (hidden vanilla mechanic)
- Displays exhaustion indicator
- Shows hunger/saturation values for food items
- Hover tooltips on food items in inventory
- Works with modded foods

## Configuration
Config file: `config/appleskin/client.toml`

Key settings:
- `showFoodValuesInTooltip` - Enable tooltips on food items
- `showSaturationHudOverlay` - Show saturation in hunger bar
- `showExhaustionHud` - Show exhaustion indicator

## Tips
- Saturation determines how long until you get hungry again
- Higher saturation foods keep you full longer
- The exhaustion bar shows when you'll lose hunger points
- Works in multiplayer servers

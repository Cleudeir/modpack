# Towns and Towers

**Version:** 1.12
**Category:** World Generation

## Description

Towns and Towers enhances vanilla villages and adds new tower structures throughout the world. Villages get more variety and detail, while new tower types provide challenging exploration and loot opportunities.

## Key Features

- Enhanced village generation with more building variety
- New tower structures in various biomes
- Improved village paths and layouts
- Better loot in village chests
- More NPC interactions and village life

## Configuration

Config file: `config/towns_and_towers.toml`

Key settings:
- Village spawn frequency
- Tower spawn rates
- Individual structure toggles

## Tips

- New villages are larger and more detailed than vanilla
- Towers often contain good mid-game loot
- Check around villages for nearby towers
- Works well with other village-enhancing mods

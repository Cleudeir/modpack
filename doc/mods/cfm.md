# MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod

**Version:** 7.0.0-pre36
**Category:** Building & Decoration

## Description
MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod is a classic furniture mod that adds functional furniture items to Minecraft. It's been around for years and is one of the most popular furniture mods available, with functional items that can be interacted with.

## Key Features
- Functional kitchen appliances (fridge, stove, sink)
- Beds and sofas
- Desks, tables, and chairs
- Lighting (lamps, ceiling fans)
- Bathroom items (toilets, bathtubs)
- Electronics (TV, computer, printer)
- Functional storage (cabinets, shelves)
- Outdoor furniture (grills, patio sets)

## Configuration
Config file: `config/cfm.toml`
- Toggle individual blocks
- Adjust interaction behaviors

## Tips
- Many items are functional (click to open fridge, turn on sink, etc.)
- Popular mod with many addon mods available
- Great for kitchen and bathroom builds

# Alex's Mobs

**Version:** 1.22.9
**Category:** Mobs & Entities

## Description
Alex's Mobs adds over 80 new creatures to Minecraft, each with unique behaviors, animations, and drops. The mod focuses on realism and immersion, adding animals from around the world along with mythical creatures.

## Key Features
- 80+ new mobs with custom AI and behaviors
- Unique drops and craftable items from each mob
- New food items, tools, and weapons
- Custom spawn rules per biome
- Eagle, Grizzly Bear, Elephant, Racoon, and many more
- Mythical creatures like the Cosmic Dragon and Shadow Fenrir
- Cross-compatibility with other mods

## Configuration
Config file: `config/alexsmobs.toml`

Key settings:
- Mob spawn weights and frequencies
- Individual mob enable/disable
- Drop multipliers
- Taming mechanics

## Tips
- Check spawn rates in your biome if mobs aren't appearing
- Many mobs have taming mechanics—check the wiki for details
- Some drops are required for endgame crafting

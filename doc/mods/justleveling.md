# Just Leveling

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Category:** Magic & RPG

## Description
A character leveling system that adds RPG-style progression with experience points, levels, and stat improvements. Players level up by gaining experience from various activities.

## Key Features
- Character leveling with XP requirements
- Stat improvements per level
- Customizable stat gains
- Level-based unlocks and rewards
- Integration with other progression mods

## Configuration
Config files are located in `config/justleveling/`. Key settings include:
- XP requirements per level
- Stat gain amounts and types
- Level caps and scaling
- Unlock conditions and rewards

## Tips
- Focus on activities that give the most XP for your build
- Balance stat gains based on your playstyle
- Some unlocks require specific level thresholds
- Check the leveling menu for available stat points

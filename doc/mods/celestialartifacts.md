# Celestial Artifacts

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Category:** Magic & RPG

## Description
A mod that adds magical artifacts with unique abilities and enchantments. Players can discover, craft, and upgrade powerful artifacts that provide special powers and bonuses.

## Key Features
- Various magical artifacts with unique effects
- Artifact upgrading and enhancement system
- Discovery through exploration and crafting
- Special abilities and passive bonuses
- Integration with other magic mods

## Configuration
Config files are located in `config/celestialartifacts/`. Key settings include:
- Artifact spawn rates and locations
- Upgrade recipes and materials
- Ability parameters and cooldowns
- Rarity settings

## Tips
- Explore thoroughly to find rare artifacts
- Upgrade artifacts at special crafting stations
- Some artifacts have synergies with other mods
- Check JEI for artifact recipes and uses

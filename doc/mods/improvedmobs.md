# Improved Mobs

**Version:** 1.20.1-2.1.0
**Category:** Mobs & Entities

## Description
Improved Mobs enhances vanilla mob AI and gives them the ability to use equipment. Mobs can spawn with armor and weapons, making combat more challenging and rewarding.

## Key Features
- Mobs can spawn with random equipment
- Improved mob pathfinding and AI
- Better combat behaviors
- Equipment drops from equipped mobs
- Configurable equipment drop rates
- Works with modded equipment

## Configuration
Config file: `config/improvedmobs.toml`

Key settings:
- Equipment spawn chance
- AI improvement levels
- Drop rate multipliers
- Mob-specific settings

## Tips
- Zombies can spawn with diamond gear on higher difficulties
- Skeletons may use shields and better bows
- Check mob equipment before engaging
- Equipment drops are affected by looting enchantment

# Twilight Forest

**Version:** 4.3.2145
**Category:** World Generation

## Description

Twilight Forest adds an entire new dimension accessible through a portal made of water and flowers. The dimension features its own progression system with bosses that must be defeated in order, unique dungeons, and a dark fantasy atmosphere.

## Key Features

- New dimension with unique biomes, mobs, and structures
- Boss progression system (Naga, Lich, Hydrolion, Ur-Ghast, Knight Phantom, Final Boss)
- Themed dungeons and towers for each boss area
- Unique tools, armor, and items not found in the Overworld
- Large trees, mushrooms, and dark forest biomes

## Configuration

Config file: `config/twilightforest.toml`

Key settings:
- Portal creation method (default: water + flowers)
- Dimension ID
- Mob spawning rules
- Boss health and difficulty

## Tips

- Craft a Twilight Map to navigate the dimension
- Progress through bosses in order for the best experience
- Bring plenty of supplies - the dimension is dangerous
- The portal requires a 2x2 water source surrounded by flowers/plants

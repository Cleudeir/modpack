# Born in Chaos

**Version:** 1.4.0
**Category:** Mobs & Entities

## Description
Born in Chaos is a horror-themed mod that adds terrifying new mobs and mechanics. The mod creates a darker, more atmospheric experience with creatures that stalk players in darkness.

## Key Features
- New hostile mobs with horror-themed designs
- Night-specific spawning mechanics
- Unique sound effects and ambient horror sounds
- Custom boss encounters
- Dark atmospheric effects
- Items and tools crafted from defeated horrors

## Configuration
Config file: `config/borninchaos.toml`

Key settings:
- Mob spawn rates and restrictions
- Horror event frequency
- Sound volume adjustments
- Difficulty scaling

## Tips
- Light levels affect spawn rates significantly
- Some mobs only spawn during specific moon phases
- Bring torches—the darkness is your enemy
- Headphones recommended for atmosphere

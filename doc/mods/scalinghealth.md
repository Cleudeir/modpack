# Scaling Health

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Category:** Magic & RPG

## Description
A mod that scales player and mob health based on difficulty and progression. It adds a dynamic difficulty system with health bars, healing mechanics, and challenge scaling.

## Key Features
- Dynamic health scaling for players and mobs
- Custom health bars and UI elements
- Healing mechanics with configurable rates
- Difficulty-based scaling options
- Integration with other difficulty mods

## Configuration
Config files are located in `config/scalinghealth/`. Key settings include:
- Health scaling formulas and curves
- Healing rate and mechanics
- Mob health multipliers
- Difficulty progression settings

## Tips
- Adjust scaling to match your desired difficulty
- Use healing items strategically as health scales
- Check health bars for enemy difficulty indicators
- Combine with other difficulty mods for enhanced challenge

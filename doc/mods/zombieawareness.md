# Zombie Awareness

**Version:** 1.20.1-2.1.0
**Category:** Mobs & Entities

## Description
Zombie Awareness makes zombies smarter by giving them better pathfinding, the ability to break doors, and enhanced awareness of players. Zombies can detect players through sound and light.

## Key Features
- Improved zombie pathfinding
- Door breaking capability
- Sound-based player detection
- Light-based awareness
- Group spawning mechanics
- Enhanced zombie behaviors

## Configuration
Config file: `config/zombieawareness.cfg`

Key settings:
- Door breaking toggle
- Sound detection range
- Light sensitivity
- Group spawn rates
- AI improvement levels

## Tips
- Zombies will break down doors to reach players
- Sound from furnaces and other machines attracts zombies
- Light management becomes crucial for base security
- Works well with other difficulty mods

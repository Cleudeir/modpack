# Sound Physics Remastered

**Version:** 1.4.x
**Category:** Quality of Life

## Description
Sound Physics Remastered adds realistic sound propagation and reverberation to Minecraft. Sounds echo in caves, muffle through walls, and behave more realistically based on the environment.

## Key Features
- Realistic sound reverberation
- Sound occlusion through blocks
- Environmental sound effects
- Echo in caves and large spaces
- Works with all sound sources

## Configuration
Config file: `config/soundphysics.toml`

Key settings:
- `reverbEnabled` - Enable reverb effects
- `occlusionEnabled` - Enable sound occlusion
- `reverbIntensity` - Strength of reverb
- `occlusionIntensity` - Strength of occlusion

## Tips
- Greatly improves cave exploration atmosphere
- Sounds are muffled through walls realistically
- Can impact performance slightly
- Pairs well with Presence Footsteps
- Disable if sound issues occur

# Ambient Sounds

**Version:** 5.x.x
**Category:** Quality of Life

## Description
Ambient Sounds adds environmental background sounds to Minecraft, making the world feel more alive with birds, wind, water, and other natural sounds based on your location.

## Key Features
- Environmental background sounds
- Biome-specific ambience
- Weather-based sounds
- Day/night sound variations
- Volume and category controls

## Configuration
Config file: `config/ambientsounds.toml`

Key settings:
- `volume` - Overall ambient volume
- `indoorAmbience` - Indoor sound effects
- `outdoorAmbience` - Outdoor sound effects
- `biomeSounds` - Biome-specific sounds

## Tips
- Greatly improves atmosphere and immersion
- Different biomes have unique sounds
- Adjust volume if too loud
- Works with most modded biomes
- Disable in areas with performance issues
